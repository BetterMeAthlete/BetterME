Lesson Transcript & Playbook ID: TM_MOD_01

"Your calendar is your executive game film. Just like coach's game film on Monday morning—it does not lie about your focus."

Most athletes don't lack raw talent or ambition; they simply bleed hours to unforced lifestyle errors because they view their schedule as a loose suggestion rather than an elite tactical operational matrix.

The 3-Color Audit Matrix:
🟢
Green Blocks (Execution): Uninterrupted hours locked entirely for court work, heavy strength training, film study, and business creation.
🟡
Yellow Blocks (Maintenance): Essential operational requirements—sleep schedules, targeted nutritional fueling, and dynamic physical therapy.
🔴
Red Blocks (Waste): Total leaks in capacity—unscripted phone use, algorithm scrolling, and passive distractions.
🎯 THE ACADEMY STANDARD: Script out tomorrow's exact Green Blocks tonight. Command the day before it starts.
