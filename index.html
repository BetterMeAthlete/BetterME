Know Myself Framework

An 11-Point Alignment Blueprint for Intentional Self-Discovery:

  • Point 01: Body Optimization & Management – Actively prioritizing rest, clean nutrition, and recovery mechanics.
  • Point 02: Elite Punctuality & Time Structures – Holding down the critical 15-minute early attendance baseline.
  • Point 03: Game Architecture as a Business – Recognizing active roles in financial/asset sports operations.
  • Point 04: Fame & Notoriety Navigation – Preparing to handle public visibility responsibly without breaking character.
  • Point 05: College & University Milestones – Organizing structural academic benchmarks for future placements.
  • Point 06: Expectations Alignment Matrix – Setting clear communication rules with teammates and coaching groups.
  • Point 07: SMART Goal Architectures – Establishing Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound plans.
  • Point 08: Structural Personal Organization – Keeping tracking logs, templates, and schedules managed.
  • Point 09: Alternative Outlets & Passion Spaces – Cultivating creative professional paths outside of sport fields.
  • Point 10: Conflict Management Protocols – Utilizing emotional regulation and active composure under high pressure.
  • Point 11: Rookie Contract Simulation – Strategically budgeting an asset distribution layout for a $6 Million contract.
