Unit 3.4: Know Myself Self-Discovery Package

This unit is different. There's no video script—it's a 1:1 session between you and the page. Answer every question honestly. No one else needs to see this. It's for you.

Complete 11-Step Portfolio Workbook

If you had to describe yourself in 3 sentences to someone who has never met you—what is there to say about you?

List your natural strengths (could be cooking, writing, communicating, drawing, listening, organizing, etc.).

Think about 6 months from now, 5 years from now, and entirely (e.g., Father, Professional Baller, Teacher, Author). If you aren't that person today, what needs to change, let go of, or keep?

What are the absolute goals you want to achieve? Think about basketball, in life as a brother or sister, as a more mature adult, etc.

Think about sports magazines, authors, or genres. Why do you like these books?

For example: ESPN, Uninterrupted, First Take, podcasts, etc.

Think about your family, friends, teammates, and coaches. What words do they use? Do you agree with them?

Think about their style of play, their character from what they show, and whether you see your own talent level in them.

What are you building toward during or after your playing days? (e.g., D-Wade co-owning a shoe brand/Jazz, LeBron running Uninterrupted media and movie production platforms).

What do you want to pass down? What are you doing today that builds that legacy up?

If you were to reach your ultimate goal, how will you feel if it doesn't provide the feeling or real satisfaction that you once thought it would give you?
